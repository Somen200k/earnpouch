# EarnPouch CPA Marketing and Content Blueprint

## Purpose

Build useful, conversion-focused CPA articles while protecting reader trust and complying with the offer provider's rules. Conversion goals never override accuracy: use only campaign terms, requirements, eligibility, rewards, and timelines confirmed by the live offer or written provider documentation.

## How CPA tracking works

1. An advertiser defines an action that may qualify for a payout.
2. A CPA network or affiliate program lists the offer, its restrictions, and attribution rules.
3. EarnPouch publishes relevant content and uses its assigned tracking link.
4. A visitor may follow the link and complete the advertiser's action.
5. The network records and validates attributed conversions under its rules; reporting and payment may be delayed or adjusted.

Do not promise that a click or action will result in a commission, that tracking is instant, or that a network acts as an escrow service unless its documentation explicitly says so.

## Offer economics

- **Payout:** the amount payable for a validated action, subject to the offer terms.
- **Conversion rate (CR):** validated conversions divided by attributed clicks. Multiply by 100 to express it as a percentage.
- **Earnings per click (EPC):** attributed earnings divided by attributed clicks. A simplified estimate is `payout × CR`, where CR is a decimal (for example, 2% = 0.02).
- **Net profit:** account for traffic acquisition costs, reversals, fees, and other operating costs; EPC alone is not profit.

Illustration: a $20 payout at a 2% conversion rate gives an estimated $0.40 EPC; a $2 payout at a 20% conversion rate also gives an estimated $0.40 EPC. These are arithmetic examples, not campaign forecasts.

Any category payout or conversion ranges supplied as planning inputs are **unverified hypotheses**, not industry benchmarks or promises. Replace them with EarnPouch's measured results by source, device, geography, and offer. A $0.30–$0.80 EPC can be used as a user-selected testing target, not a universal “sweet spot.”

Before recommending a topic or offer, ask whether its planned traffic is broad social traffic or intent-driven search traffic. Then compare verified audience restrictions, action friction, payout, measured conversion rate, EPC, and net acquisition costs. Do not choose solely by headline payout.

## Article layout

Use the shared article components and content fields in Admin Content Studio. CPA links are optional for informational articles; only add a campaign link when it is relevant, approved, and accurately disclosed.

1. A direct, accurate H1 and short opening that answer the reader's question without claiming an unverified outcome.
2. A compact offer snapshot and up to three quick checks. Describe confirmed requirements; direct readers to the live terms for details that may change.
3. A prominent CTA separated from surrounding text, with a small muted disclosure immediately below it. Keep the CTA label descriptive and consistent with the actual next step.
4. Optional longer guidance below “Read the complete guide.”

Avoid fake testimonials, fabricated screenshots, false scarcity, guaranteed prizes or earnings, unsupported “we tested it” claims, and copy that suggests a third-party campaign is official or endorsed by a named brand. Never imply that an action guarantees eligibility, selection, employment, or payment.

## Drafting blueprints

These are starting points, not claims of current availability. Verify every step with the actual provider before publishing. Do not use “official” for a third-party portal unless the provider and its role are verified.

### Smart Budgeting: household finance and budgeting

- **Working headline:** How to Compare Budgeting Tools and Plan Monthly Spending
- **Snapshot:** Budgeting tool or household finance guide
- **Checks:** Review fees and trial terms; confirm supported accounts and privacy practices; compare features against the reader's needs.
- **Next step:** Review the provider's current product terms before connecting accounts or starting a trial.
- **CTA:** Review budgeting tool
- **Disclosure direction:** Disclose any affiliate relationship and distinguish educational guidance from individualized financial advice.

The current EarnPouch navigation places its existing gift-card giveaway guides in this category at the publisher's direction. That is a site taxonomy choice, not a claim that giveaways are budgeting products. Keep future article copy clear about the promotion's actual subject.

### Reward Apps: app installs and CPI

- **Working topic:** An app-install campaign may ask a user to install a named rewards app and complete an in-app action such as installing a game.
- **Checks:** Confirm the approved tracking link, supported device and operating system, eligible geography, exact event that qualifies, payout terms, and any deadline from the live network offer.
- **Next step:** Explain the verified install and in-app steps, without promising that every install will qualify or produce a reward.
- **CTA:** Use a clear action label that matches the campaign.
- **Disclosure direction:** Identify the third-party app and disclose that eligibility, attribution, validation, and payment are controlled by the provider and network terms.

### Side Hustles: market research

- **Working headline:** Market Research Panels: Check Whether Applications Are Open
- **Snapshot:** Market research panel eligibility
- **Checks:** Check current availability in the user's area; review participant requirements; provide requested contact details only after reviewing consent and privacy terms.
- **Next step:** Review the platform's registration requirements.
- **CTA:** Check panel availability
- **Disclosure direction:** Clarify whether this is an independent directory/referral and that registration or paid participation is not guaranteed.

### Remote Work: task platforms

- **Working headline:** Remote Task Platforms: Check Registration and Work Requirements
- **Snapshot:** Online task platform guide
- **Checks:** Check whether registration is open; review device and location requirements; understand onboarding and payment terms.
- **Next step:** Review the provider's application or onboarding process.
- **CTA:** View platform requirements
- **Disclosure direction:** Do not imply that EarnPouch hires, guarantees access, or promises work or income.

### Reward apps and giveaways

- **Working headline:** Explain the specific promotion and how to review its entry details.
- **Snapshot:** Identify the offer as third-party when applicable and distinguish a chance to win from a guaranteed reward.
- **Checks:** Confirm campaign availability; review the organizer, eligibility, privacy notice, and rules; submit only information the live page requests and the reader agrees to provide.
- **Next step:** Direct readers to complete the listed steps accurately only if they choose to proceed.
- **CTA:** Continue to offer
- **Disclosure direction:** Identify the independent promotion, clarify that it is not sponsored by or affiliated with the named brand when that is accurate, and state that entry does not guarantee a prize.

Never claim “free entry,” an “official drawing page,” a verified winner-selection process, or a prize amount unless the current official rules establish it. If the terms are unavailable, say so and do not fill the gap with assumptions.

## Review checklist

- Does the offer accept the audience's country, state, age, and device?
- Is the tracking URL assigned to EarnPouch and approved for the intended traffic source?
- Do the article headline, steps, CTA, and disclosure match the current offer?
- Are any earnings, reward, eligibility, urgency, and privacy claims supported?
- Are CR and EPC clearly measured or labeled as estimates?
- Does the reader know they are leaving EarnPouch for a third-party provider?
