Reward and survey offers are often described as available in the United States, but that wording does not necessarily mean every state, device, or participant is eligible. Before you share an email address or ZIP code, check the campaign's current terms and confirm that your location fits its rules.

Eligibility can differ between providers and even between campaigns on the same site. Use the exact offer page as the source of truth rather than relying on an old review, search snippet, or another person's experience.

## How to verify state availability

### Read the full eligibility section

Look for accepted countries, state exclusions, age requirements, and any limits based on residency. If the page says only “US residents” but does not clarify a question that matters to you, contact the provider before submitting information.

### Check requirements on the actual campaign page

A general panel page may describe typical participation, while an individual campaign has separate limits. Read the terms attached to the activity you plan to start, including what actions qualify and whether additional steps apply.

### Confirm device and connection requirements

Some campaigns may specify a supported device, browser, or app. Follow the stated rules and use accurate location information. Do not use a VPN, proxy, or another person's ZIP code to try to bypass a restriction; this can violate the terms and cause an activity not to qualify.

### Check the details again before continuing

Offer pages and availability can change. Review the campaign terms immediately before you submit or begin an activity. Save a copy of the relevant terms if you want a record of what was shown when you joined.

## What a ZIP-code form can—and cannot—tell you

A provider may request a ZIP code as part of location screening or study recruitment. Entering it does not guarantee that the campaign is available, that you will pass screening, or that you will receive a reward. Additional eligibility questions may follow.

Only share your location details after you identify the provider and understand its privacy notice. If the purpose of the request is unclear, or the site does not provide understandable terms, it is reasonable not to continue.

## Check the complete offer, not just the headline

Before deciding, review:

- Whether your state and age group are included.
- Whether the offer requires surveys, purchases, subscriptions, or other partner actions.
- What personal information is requested and how it may be used.
- What counts as completion and what exclusions apply.
- Whether a reward is offered, and any conditions, thresholds, or timing.
- How to contact the provider if the terms or eligibility are unclear.

Avoid relying on “up to” amounts or a quick signup description as proof of a payout. The provider's current written terms determine whether an activity qualifies and what, if anything, may be issued.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why might a campaign exclude some states?

Campaigns can have their own geographic eligibility rules. The provider should identify applicable restrictions in its current terms or explain them through official support.

### If the offer page accepts my ZIP code, am I approved?

Not necessarily. A form accepting an entry is not the same as final eligibility approval or completion of the campaign's requirements.

### What if I cannot find the state's eligibility rules?

Do not guess. Ask the provider using its official support channel or choose not to submit your information until the rules are clear.

## The bottom line

Check the exact offer's country and state terms before you start. Accurate location details and a clear understanding of the steps help you avoid spending time on an offer that is not available or suitable for you.
