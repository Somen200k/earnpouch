## 1. What is the FindResourcesUSA Questionnaire?

FindResourcesUSA operates an independent, private web portal that provides informational resources to US residents. 

Rather than functioning as a direct application portal, the site offers a **short screening questionnaire**. Users answer basic questions about their household, and the platform generates a personalized informational guide outlining potential relief options, budget strategies, and general assistance programs that the user *may* want to look into.

**Important Distinction:** FindResourcesUSA is **not** a government service. It is a private company. Using their site is an educational stepping stone, not a substitute for an official state application.
