## What types of projects might appear?

OneForma’s public project listings can cover different kinds of participation. Depending on what is open, examples may include collecting data, reviewing language or search results, recording content, or applying subject-matter knowledge. Not every category is open at the same time, and a listing may be limited to a particular country, language, location, or skill set.

Treat each listing as its own opportunity. A US project may ask for specific equipment, a particular language, professional credentials, a certain location, or a defined schedule. A project visible on the site does not mean every US applicant can join.

## How to evaluate a project listing

Before applying or sharing information, check:

### Location and qualification requirements

Look for the project’s eligible countries or states, age rules, language proficiency, required experience, and any tests or assessments. If the listing says it is open in the United States, read the full detail for local exclusions or additional requirements.

### Tasks and time commitment

Understand what you will actually do, whether work is remote or on-site, what equipment is needed, and whether there are deadlines or scheduled sessions. A project title alone may not explain the full time commitment.

### Compensation and approval

Read the project’s current compensation details and the conditions for accepted work. Confirm how completion is reviewed, whether rejected submissions are possible, and what payment timing and method the project states. Do not assume pay rates from old forum posts or a different project apply.

### Data collection and privacy

Some projects may involve voice, video, images, or other personal data. Review the consent language and privacy details to understand what is collected, how it may be used, and whether it can be deleted. Do not submit recordings or identity details until you understand the project’s requirements.

### Official communication and fees

Use OneForma’s official site and account area to review and apply. Be cautious of people impersonating a recruiter or asking for unexplained fees, passwords, payment PINs, or login codes. Verify unusual requests using official support information.

## Does OneForma guarantee US work?

No platform overview can guarantee that a specific project is open to you. Listings and demand may change; applicants may need to pass screening or training, and project availability can be limited. Check the live OneForma project page for current US eligibility and apply only when the requirements match your situation.

Consider whether the work’s schedule, equipment, and stated compensation fit your needs. If you need predictable hours or guaranteed income, project-based work may not be a substitute for regular employment.
