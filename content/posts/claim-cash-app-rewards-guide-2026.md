## How third-party payment-app promotions may work

Some marketing campaigns use prize drawings or other promotions to attract interest and invite consumers to submit contact details. Depending on the organizer and its disclosures, information may be used to administer an entry, check location eligibility, or send promotional communications. These practices vary, and EarnPouch has not independently verified the organizer's business model or data practices for this specific campaign.

Before entering, read the live form language, privacy notice, and official rules. Check who collects your email and ZIP code, whether information may be shared with partners, whether you are opting into marketing, and whether the form leads to any additional offers. Do not assume that providing contact details only registers an entry.

## Check the campaign's eligibility and prize terms

The offer listing reviewed by EarnPouch specifies Apple iOS users in the United States and indicates that email and ZIP code are requested. Device and location targeting may vary or change, so use the current offer page and official rules to confirm:

- Which locations and age groups are eligible.
- Whether the campaign is currently open and when it ends.
- What steps count as a valid entry and whether limits apply.
- Who sponsors and administers the promotion.
- What the prize actually is, how many are available, and how a winner is selected.
- How a potential winner is contacted and how the prize is claimed.

Do not infer that a “Cash App gift card” means a Cash App account balance or direct deposit. The rules should state the exact prize and delivery method. If these details or the organizer's identity are unclear, do not submit information.

## Cash App security: common scam warning signs

Cash App and other payment services are frequent targets for impersonation and payment scams. Keep these safeguards in mind:

- **Cash flipping promises:** Claims that someone can multiply a small payment into a larger one are a common scam pattern. Do not send money to someone promising to “flip” your Cash App funds.
- **Requests for credentials:** Never share your Cash App PIN, password, one-time login code, or bank login with a promotion page, recruiter, or person messaging you.
- **Fees to release a prize:** Be cautious of messages claiming you won but must pay a clearance, verification, tax, or release fee directly to the sender. Check the official rules and verify the organizer independently.
- **Impersonation:** Scammers may copy logos, use similar names, or contact people from unofficial accounts. Use the official Cash App app or website to verify anything involving your account.
- **Unclear entry terms:** A trustworthy promotion should make the organizer, rules, privacy practices, and entry requirements available. Pause if those details are missing or contradictory.

Do not treat a promotion using Cash App branding as an official Cash App offer. Check the sponsor and administrator named in the campaign rules.
