Holiday promotions promising a $1,000 Cash App gift card can sound exciting—and it’s sensible to ask who is behind the offer. The listing linked here describes a **chance for a reward**, not a guaranteed $1,000 transfer, and it doesn’t establish that Cash App runs or endorses the campaign.

Before sharing information, check the live page and official rules. They should tell you who operates the promotion, who can participate, what steps are required, and what the advertised prize actually is.

## First, find out how the promotion works

The offer is a third-party campaign, separate from Cash App’s official financial services. The supplied listing describes a chance to receive a digital cash reward, but it doesn’t confirm whether the promotion is a sweepstakes or a program that requires completing sponsor activities.

Those arrangements are different: a sweepstakes awards a prize by chance under its rules, while a reward program may have specific tasks or other conditions. Don’t assume which one this is based on the headline—the campaign rules should explain the format and requirements.

## What to check in the fine print

Before entering an email address or other information, look for:

- **The organizer:** Find the sponsor and administrator in the rules. A Cash App name or logo alone doesn’t prove that Cash App or Block, Inc. runs the campaign.
- **Eligibility:** Confirm location, age limits, entry period, and exclusions.
- **Entry requirements:** Check whether a form is enough or if other activities are required. Read any partner-offer terms before signing up.
- **Privacy and consent:** See how your information may be used or shared and whether you’re opting into marketing.
- **Prize details:** Look for the exact prize, how a winner is selected and notified, and how the prize is delivered.

If the rules or privacy notice are missing or unclear, it’s reasonable to skip the offer. Never share your Cash App PIN, password, bank login, or one-time security code to enter or claim a prize.

## Watch for payment scams

Scammers may pretend you’ve won and ask you to send money to release the prize. Don’t pay an unexpected “clearance” or “release” fee through Cash App, gift card, or another payment method. Verify any claim through the promotion’s official rules and the organizer’s independently located contact details.
